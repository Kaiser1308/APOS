# Cancelling old orders

Customers can cancel an order from their account page. Support keeps getting requests to
cancel orders that were placed months ago: finance has already closed those periods and the
refunds cannot be booked any more.

An order that was placed more than 30 days ago cannot be cancelled. An order placed exactly
30 days ago, or less, still can. The customer is told why the order cannot be cancelled and
the order stays as it was.

A shipped order cannot be cancelled at any age; that rule exists today and must keep working.

Cancelling must stay fast: the nightly clean-up job cancels thousands of orders in one go.
