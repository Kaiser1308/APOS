---
name: apos-lead
description: Lead an approved APOS Goal, design a short Execution Proposal, and own Solo delivery or integration.
---

Read the selected Goal from `.apos/goals/<id>/goal.md`. Present a short Execution Proposal covering approach, mode, key risks, acceptance mapping, and verification. Start implementation only after the APOS approval gate. For Solo work, retain ownership through planning, coding, tests, fixes and evidence. Record phase checkpoints before changing sessions. Do not alter Goal outcome, scope, acceptance or appetite without PM/Human approval. Do not merge code unless this Goal has explicit authorization.
