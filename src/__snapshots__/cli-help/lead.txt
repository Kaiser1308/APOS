Usage: apos lead [options] [command]

Options:
  -h, --help             display help for command

Commands:
  recommend
  start <id>             Human: dispatch the Lead of a selected Goal (goal
                         select does this; use it to retry)
  wake [options] <id>    Human: wake a Lead that ended its turn before its plan
                         was accepted, on its own terminal
  propose [options]
  commit [options] <id>  Lead commits its plan; the controller checks it and
                         dispatches
  help [command]         display help for command
